A dark 2D action platformer developed by Black Mermaid and released on PC and major console platforms.

Role

Co-Founder / Lead Unity Developer / Sole Programmer. Responsible for the technical development of the game from early prototype through commercial release and post-launch support.

Responsibilities

  • Owned the Unity codebase as the sole programmer.

  • Provided technical leadership for a multidisciplinary team of 3-9 members.
  • Supported the game after launch with updates, bug fixes, and maintenance.
